## Short Description

A local-first Chrome extension that helps you pause before distracting searches, URLs, and browsing patterns.

## Product Description

OakenShield is a personal focus extension for Chrome. Add your distraction keywords, browse normally, and OakenShield will create a moment of friction when it detects a matching search, URL, or form input.

It is built for people who do not want a heavy blocker, account system, or cloud dashboard. OakenShield runs locally in your browser, stores data locally, and gives you a simple dashboard for focus stats and domain/time summaries.
